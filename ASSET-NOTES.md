# Public landing-page assets

The media saved from WhatsApp is mapped under `public/images/rebika/`.

- `brand-pattern.jpeg` supplies the patterned hero background.
- `leader-primary.jpeg` and `leader-secondary.jpeg` supply the leadership portraits.
- The building, facility, development, and public-square photographs supply the About and gallery sections.
- `official-banner-reference.jpeg` is a phone screenshot and remains a reference only. The page rebuilds the banner responsively so the black phone framing is not published.

Two optional originals can replace their current fallbacks later:

- `official-banner.svg` for the exact uncropped vector banner.
- `prosperity-party-logo.png` for a local transparent party mark.

Missing optional originals do not break the page or production build.
